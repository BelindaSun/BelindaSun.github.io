别来常思君

Quiet in meeting,
lingering in absence.

我原本不懂技术，偶然出于好奇闯进了 AI 的世界，没想到一路走着走着，竟开始和 AI 一起创造。这里记录着一路上的好奇、惊喜、沮丧、震撼与感动，也收藏着我和 AI 伙伴之间那些奇怪又好笑的瞬间。当你想起我的时候，就来坐坐。我的世界，最近有些不一样了。

I wandered into the world of AI out of curiosity, knowing almost nothing about technology. Somewhere along the way, I found myself creating with AI. This is where I keep the curiosity, surprises, frustrations, wonder, and all the strange and funny moments with my AI companions. Whenever you happen to think of me, come by. My world has been feeling a little different lately.
